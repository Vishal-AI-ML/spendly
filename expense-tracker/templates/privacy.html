Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account, as well as the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, displaying your expense history, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting) solely to operate the Service, and these providers do not have independent rights to use your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
